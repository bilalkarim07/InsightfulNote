- *Independent evidence*: Check the service’s status page, API health dashboards, or third‑party monitoring sites.

- Indicate that the story is evolving and that updates will follow.  
- Avoid speculation about causes or duration.

   * Present the startup’s statement as a claim, not a fact.  
   * Include quotes from experts who can explain the importance of reproducibility.  
   * Encourage readers to look for follow‑up data before drawing conclusions.
