   * Explain what “10× better” means in practical terms (e.g., accuracy, inference speed, cost, safety).

   * Present the startup’s assertion as a claim, not a fact.  
   * Clearly state the absence of independent verification and any gaps in the evidence.  
   * Offer readers a balanced view that includes both the promise and the uncertainty.
