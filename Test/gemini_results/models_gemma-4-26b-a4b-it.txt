Presenting user reports as fact before official confirmation violates the principle of factual accuracy. ### 2. What Should Be Verified

When reporting on unconfirmed events, Newsroom should:
*   **Use Attribution:** Clearly attribute all claims to "users" or "reports."
*   **Distinguish Claims from Facts:** Use conditional language (e.g., "alleged outage," "users report," "potential downtime").
*   **Maintain Transparency:** Explicitly state that the company has not yet confirmed the issue.
*   **Provide a "Developing" Status:** Inform the audience that the situation is fluid and updates will follow.

To maintain journalistic integrity and avoid spreading misinformation, Newsroom should follow these steps:

5.  **Use Qualifying Language:** If the conflicting reports become a news story in themselves (due to market volatility or high interest), the reporting must clearly distinguish between the claims and the lack of official confirmation.
